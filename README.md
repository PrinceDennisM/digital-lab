# Digital Lab

This is my personal digital laboratory.

A place to experiment, build, break things, learn, and turn ideas into working projects.

## Principle

Curiosity first.

Build first.

Learn by doing.

## What I Want This Lab To Become

A place where I can turn curiosity into experiments,
experiments into working prototypes,
and prototypes into things that can exist in the real world.

AI is not here to replace my thinking.

It is here to amplify it.
